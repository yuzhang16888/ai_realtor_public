Title: 2025 Buyer Agent Commission Changes
- Starting in 2025, buyers may pay their own agent directly.
- Pricing models: percentage, flat fee, or à la carte (CMA, offer drafting, disclosure review).
- Always include: Not legal/financial advice. Encourage inspections and loan pre-approval.
- Multiple-offer markets (SF Bay Area, NYC, Boston): flat fee per offer can reduce total cost.
